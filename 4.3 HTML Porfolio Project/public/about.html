About Me

IT Support Specialist | Customer Service Professional | Aspiring Software Engineer | Bilingual (English/Spanish)

I'm a tech-savvy, people-first professional with a passion for solving problems and delivering exceptional experiences — whether that's resolving a critical IT issue for a city department, helping a customer find exactly what they need, or building something from scratch in code.

With 3+ years of experience as an Information Technology Specialist for the City of Manhattan Beach, I managed 400+ endpoints across 9 departments, served as the primary help desk contact, and kept technology running smoothly for an entire municipal government. I'm experienced in Active Directory, Microsoft 365, Jamf MDM, endpoint deployment, and ticketing systems — but what I'm most proud of is my ability to take complex technical problems and turn them into simple, clear solutions for the people I support.

Outside of IT, I bring hands-on experience in retail operations and customer service through my work at Whole Foods Market, where I've developed a sharp eye for detail, a strong sense of urgency, and a genuine commitment to creating a positive experience for every customer I interact with.

I also work as a Math and Science tutor, which reflects something core to who I am — I love helping people grow, whether that's a student mastering calculus, a colleague learning a new system, or a customer discovering something new.

Beyond my day-to-day work, I'm on an intentional path toward software engineering and web development. My progress and personal projects live on my GitHub (github.com/franciscojrivera), where I actively push code and document what I'm learning.
